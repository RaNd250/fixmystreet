Help: frequently asked questions

What is [% site_name %]?
A site for reporting problems and obstacles in public spaces in Greece: potholes, dumped rubbish and rubble, broken pavements, street lights out, fallen trees, illegal parking. You mark the place on the map and the report is sent to the responsible municipality.
How do I make a report?
  1. On the home page, type an address or area (or allow your location) and click the place on the map.
  2. Choose the category of the problem.
  3. Add one or more photos, from your gallery or with “Take photo”, and describe what is wrong.
  4. Fill in your details and press “Submit”.
If you leave the page by mistake, what you wrote is waiting for you when you come back to the form, in the same tab.
Do I need an account?
No. The report is sent straight away, with no account and no confirmation. If you give an email, we send you a personal link (valid for 24 hours) to create an account, if you wish, and follow your reports.
Can I report anonymously?
Yes. Your name is not shown on the site unless you tick “Show my name publicly”. The email is optional as long as your name is not shown; without an email, though, you will not hear what happened to the report. Any name and phone number you give go only to the responsible service.
Where does my report go?
To the municipality where the place is. Exceptions:
  • Parking at an electric vehicle charger, or blocking the way (garage entrance, double parking, bus stops): it goes as a complaint to the local police station and traffic police. These complaints are not published on the site. After sending it you can also text it to 100, with the message ready, from your phone.
  • Cones or obstacles put out by a shop, unauthorised markings with a P-72 sign: these go to the municipality, which deals with the use of public space. The form also suggests reporting it on gov.gr’s MyStreet.
  • Parking on a disabled ramp or bay, on the pavement or on a crossing: these are recorded by Χωρίς Εμπόδια (xorisempodia.gr), and the form takes you there.
What happens to the photos?
Number plates and faces are blurred automatically before a photo is stored; that is the copy everyone sees and the one sent to the municipality. Only in complaints to the police does the photo go with the plate readable (faces always blurred), and that copy is deleted as soon as the complaint is sent. If a plate is read in the photo, it is filled in on the form for you; please check it.
What is [% site_name %] not for?
It is not an emergency service. For anything urgent call 112, the police on 100, the fire service on 199 or an ambulance on 166. It is also not for complaints about people, disputes between neighbours, or anything outside public space.
Who sees my report?
Reports to municipalities are shown on the map, so that they are not reported again and so that it can be seen where a problem keeps coming back. You can say that you agree (👍) or disagree (👎) with a report, or add an update, e.g. that the problem has been fixed. Complaints to the police are not shown.
What about inappropriate words?
They are replaced with ***. A report or update with many such words is neither published nor sent until we have checked it.
How do I find a report?
Type its number (e.g. 25102026-1) in the search box on the home page. Before you send a new report, the form shows whether an open report or complaint is already within 100 m, so the same one is not sent twice.
Can I crop a photo?
Yes. Once you pick or take a photo a frame opens: drag it or its corners to keep only what is needed and press “Crop”, or “Whole photo” to send it as it is.
How much does it cost?
Nothing. The site is free, with no advertising.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes, in your phone’s browser. You can also add it to your home screen (“Add to Home screen” or “Install app”) so that it opens like an app.

For municipalities and services

Which address do you send reports to?
The email address each municipality publishes (for some, one per category), or the local police station and traffic police for parking complaints. If it is not the right one, send us the right address from the contact page or as described at the end of each message.
How do we reply to the citizen?
Reply to the email itself: the reply goes straight to the citizen who made the report, or to us if they gave no email.

About the site

Who made [% site_name %]?
It is built on mySociety’s open source FixMyStreet platform. This site’s code is open source, under the GNU AGPL v3: source code.
How do I contact you?
From the contact page. Please do not send reports from there; the report form is on the home page.
