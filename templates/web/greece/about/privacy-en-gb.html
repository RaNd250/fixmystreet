Privacy policy

In plain words: what we need, where it goes, and what you can ask for.

Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. Who is responsible

The controller is [% c.config.GREECE_CONTROLLER_NAME | html %][% IF c.config.GREECE_CONTROLLER_ADDRESS %] ([% c.config.GREECE_CONTROLLER_ADDRESS | html %])[% END %]. The team of volunteers that runs [% site_name %] acts under the instructions of [% c.config.GREECE_CONTROLLER_NAME | html %]. To exercise your rights you can contact [% c.config.GREECE_CONTROLLER_NAME | html %] or use the contact form. For any question or request write to [% c.cobrand.contact_email | html %].

2. What we collect

With every report: the place on the map and the address found for it, the category, the answers to the category's questions, the description, the photos and the date. If you edit the description of one of your reports, we keep the text before each change for 3 months (only moderators see it), and for good the note that the report was edited, when and by whom.

If you add a video: up to 45 seconds. We process it on our own server (720p, without location or device details) and it is not published: only the accounts of the service that receives the report see it, inside the platform. It is not sent by email.

If you give them: name, email and phone. Email and phone are optional; without an email and phone the report is sent without contact details. A name (at least 4 characters) is needed when the report has no photo.

With an account: email, name and password (stored only hashed) or, if you sign in with Google, the identifier and email the provider gives us.

Technical: IP address, browser type and request times in the server logs, for security and fixing faults.

3. Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • To get the report to the responsible service and show it on the map: legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), in having problems in public space recorded and fixed. The legitimate interest rests on the public benefit of recording and resolving problems in public space. The personal data that is published is limited (the point, the category, the description, blurred photos) and you can ask for it to be anonymised.
  • To show your name publicly: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), with the “Show my name” box; you can withdraw it from your account.
  • To send you, if you gave an email, news of the report and the account link: the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Your device’s location: only if you allow it in the browser.
  • Aggregate statistics by municipality and category and protecting the service from spam and abuse: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

We do not sell data and do no ad targeting.

4. Who receives the report

The report is emailed to the service responsible for the place and the category, usually the municipality or, for some categories, the Hellenic Police. With it go the place and address, the category, the description, the photos and the date and, only if you gave them, your name and phone; your email only if you confirm it with the code we send you (the service can then reply to you directly). If you give no email and no phone, the report is sent without contact details.

Number plates and faces in photos are blurred automatically, on our own server. Only reports to the police also carry the photo with the plate readable, because without it the report cannot be examined.

5. What is public

The map and the report page show the place, the category, the description, the blurred photos, the date and the updates. Email and phone are never shown; your name only if you choose. Complaints about parking and obstacles are shown without a name, and number plates in the description are replaced with ***; the plate goes only to the police or the municipality.

Do not write names, number plates or other details of other people in the description. If a published report shows something that should not be visible, you can hide it at once from its page (the “Hide for privacy reasons” link); we will review it afterwards.

6. Cookies and browser storage

We use only what the site needs to work; no advertising or analytics cookies. That is why we do not ask for consent with a banner.

  • a sign-in cookie, while you are signed in;
  • a cookie with a random identifier, so your vote (👍/👎) on a report counts once;
  • a cookie (gr_terms, one year) that remembers you accepted the terms of use, so we do not ask on every report. With each report we also keep which version of the terms you accepted and when (needed as proof of the acceptance);
  • the draft of the report you are writing, only in your browser, so it is not lost if you leave by mistake; it is cleared once the report is sent;
  • whatever the Cloudflare Turnstile security check needs.

7. Providers

  • Cloudflare: the connection to the site passes through its network, and Turnstile checks you are not a bot before a form is sent.
  • Photon (Komoot): when you type an address in the search, we send it only the text you typed, not your IP address or cookies.
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim: to find the address of a place, our server sends its coordinates, with nothing else.
  • MapIt (mySociety): where the municipalities' boundaries come from. We mostly work them out locally; when a point is very close to a boundary or the coast and cannot be decided locally, only the coordinates of that point are sent to it, with nothing else.
  • Google: only if you choose to sign in with it.

Some providers (in particular Cloudflare) may process data outside the European Economic Area. In those cases appropriate safeguards under Article 46 GDPR (Standard Contractual Clauses) are used.

The map is drawn by our own server, from OpenStreetMap data.

8. How long we keep it

We keep the map history apart from the personal data of each report.

  • Map history: the place, the category, the municipality, the state, the dates and the services' replies stay, so it shows over time where and how often problems occur. They hold nothing that identifies you.
  • The personal data of a report (name, phone, photos, free text, citizens' updates): 24 months after the last activity on the report. Then the report is anonymised: only its category remains, and the photos are removed by the daily clean-up.
  • Accounts: as long as you use them. If you do not sign in for 24 months, we email you 30 days before and then the account is anonymised. If you ask for deletion, it happens sooner.
  • Reports you did not confirm: 14 days. Sign-in links and codes: up to 7 days. Alerts that were disabled or never confirmed: 90 days.
  • Photos: those uploaded without joining a report are removed in 7 days. The copy with a readable plate (for the police only) is deleted as soon as the complaint is sent, and in any case within 30 days.
  • Earlier texts of reports you edited: 3 months from the change. Only the note «edited», with date and time, remains.
  • Videos: 6 months after the report; those uploaded without a report being sent, one day.
  • Messages from the contact form and emails that bounced: 90 days.
  • Logs (IP address, time, request): at most three files of 10 MB per service, which wear out and are replaced as new traffic arrives; a few MB are written a day, so they cover a few days to a few weeks. The logs of the automatic clean-ups (report numbers, recipients' addresses) are kept 8 weeks.

What has already been emailed to a municipality or the police we cannot recall; the service that received it handles it, under its own retention rules. If you ask for deletion, we anonymise or delete your personal details from the site (your rights).

9. Security

  • All traffic to the site is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are stored only hashed; nobody, including us, can read them.
  • Accounts of municipalities, other bodies and administrators sign in only with a password and a second step: a one-time code sent to their email.
  • Plates and faces are blurred on our own server before a photo is stored. The copy with the plate readable, only for complaints to the police, is deleted automatically once the complaint is sent.
  • A bot check (Cloudflare Turnstile) and limits on the number of requests.
  • Only the administrators have access to the server and the database.

No system is perfectly secure. If we find a breach that concerns you, we will tell you and notify the Hellenic Data Protection Authority, as the law requires.

10. Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, and object to processing. Write to [% c.cobrand.contact_email | html %]. From your account you can also hide your name from your reports. If you think we have not respected your rights, you can turn to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority.

11. Changes

When something changes in how the site works, we update this page and its date. See also the Terms of use.
