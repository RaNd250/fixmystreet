Terms of use

The basic rules for using [% site_name %] properly and safely.

Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. The service

[% site_name %] helps you report problems in public space: potholes, broken pavements, street lights out, cleaning and green-space issues, parking that blocks ramps, pavements, crossings or chargers. Each report is emailed to the service responsible for the place and the category, usually the municipality or, for some categories, the police. Most reports also appear on the public map.

The site may be used by people over 15. By submitting a report you declare that you are over 15 and that you have the capacity to enter into this agreement.

By using the site you accept these terms. If you do not agree, do not send reports and do not use the account features.

2. Reports

Before sending a report, make sure the place on the map and the category are right. Report only what you have seen yourself, and describe it accurately.

The report reaches the service on your behalf, but it is not an official application with a protocol number and does not replace the emergency services. For anything urgent call 112, 100 or 199.

3. With or without an account

You can report without an account. Without an email and phone the report is sent without contact details, but you will not hear what happened. If you give an email, we send you a link to open an account if you wish and follow your reports.

You can sign in with email and password or with Google. You are responsible for access to your device and your email. Accounts for municipalities and other bodies are created by us, once we have checked they belong to the body.

4. SMS to 100

For reports that go to the police, after sending you can, if you wish, open a ready SMS to 100. The message is prepared on your device and is not sent automatically: you decide whether to check it, change it or send it.

The site does not represent the Hellenic Police or any municipality and does not guarantee that a message or a report will be received, examined or lead to any particular action.

  • abusive content, harassing or targeting people, and using the site for an unlawful purpose;

  • automated mass submission and any attempt to get around the limits or security measures.

The following are expressly prohibited:

  • personal data of third parties (names, home addresses, number plates that have not been blurred, photos of recognisable people);
  • false, defamatory or misleading claims;
  • content that infringes the rights of others or the law.

The user is responsible for any damage that results from breaking the above, to the extent the law allows.

We reserve the right to remove, hide or modify any content, at any time and without notice, if in our judgement it breaks these terms or the law or puts the service or others at risk. We may also limit or stop access where there is abuse.

6. What is public

The map and the report pages may show the place, the category, the description, the photos (with number plates and faces blurred), the date, the updates and the progress of the report, as well as aggregate statistics.

Your email and phone are never shown publicly; your name only if you choose. Complaints about parking and obstacles are published without a name or number plate. More in the Privacy policy.
